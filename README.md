# Ecommerce Sales Dashboard – Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing ecommerce sales data to uncover revenue, cost, and profit insights across products, regions, and customer segments.

## 📊 Overview
This dashboard transforms raw ecommerce transaction data into a clear, interactive view for business decision-making — tracking sales performance across cities, regions, product categories, and payment methods.

## 🎯 Key Features
- **KPI Cards:** Total Sales Amount, Purchase Cost, and Profit at a glance
- **Interactive Filters:** Gender, Payment Method (COD, Credit/Debit Card, Net Banking, UPI, Wallet), and Product Category (Beauty, Books, Electronics, Fashion, Grocery, Home & Kitchen, Sports)
- **Regional Analysis:** Sales breakdown by region (Central, East, North, South, West) and by city (Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Chennai, Delhi, Jaipur, Lucknow, Mumbai, Pune, etc.)
- **Product Performance:** Amount vs. Profit trend across top products

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- **Power BI** – Dashboard design, DAX measures, and data visualization
- **Microsoft Excel** – Data cleaning and preparation

## 📈 Insights Generated
- Identified top-performing cities and regions by sales volume
- Compared revenue vs. profit trends across products to spot high-margin items
- Enabled quick filtering by payment method and customer demographics for targeted analysis
